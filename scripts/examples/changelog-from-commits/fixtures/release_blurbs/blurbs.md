# Release blurbs · Tallyboard v2.5.0

All copy below is derived from `format_changelog/CHANGELOG.md`. Nothing here
claims more than the changelog does.

## App store "What's New" (under 500 characters)

Tasks that repeat on their own, search that looks inside your PDF and Word
attachments, and a keyboard shortcut sheet (press ?). Large boards now sync
about 2x faster, drag and drop works better on touch screens, and we fixed
early reminders after daylight saving changes plus a handful of other bugs.
Changing your password now signs out your other devices.

## Email update

**Subject:** Tallyboard 2.5: recurring tasks and smarter search

- **Recurring tasks**: set it once and the next one appears when you finish.
- **Search inside attachments**: find words inside PDFs and Word files.
- **Faster sync**: large boards sync about 2x faster.

Using our API? The legacy v1 webhooks endpoint is gone in this release. Move
to v2 before you upgrade: https://tallyboard.example/docs/webhooks

Full release notes: https://tallyboard.example/changelog
